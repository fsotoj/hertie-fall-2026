The other required paper

Andersen, Dustmann and Landersø (2019), Lowering Welfare Benefits: Intended and Unintended Consequences for Migrants and their Families, CReAM discussion paper 05/19.

The March 2019 abstract describes Denmark's Start Aid benefit cut for refugee immigrants. It reports higher short-run earnings and employment alongside lower household income, more property crime and adverse child outcomes. This is an abstract-based orientation; verify the estimates and identification in the assigned paper.
