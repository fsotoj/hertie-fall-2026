What changed, and what it costs

done Maths lab → C23-Lab3 Thu 12:00 becomes Wed 18:00 · 3.30
unchanged Data Science stays E1339 Thu 12:00 · Forum (A+B)
unchanged Its lab stays Monday E1339-Lab · Mon 16:00

Thursday is clear, which was the point. The price is that the week stays four days rather than three: Monday keeps the Data Science lab, and Wednesday now stretches from 10:00 to 20:00 with a four-hour hole in the afternoon.

Moving to the C11 slot is still available and would still collapse the week onto Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, since the Data Science lab follows its slot and would land on Monday noon or Tuesday afternoon. It is no longer a fix for anything, though. It is a preference about shape.

Still to ask

The schedule closed the dates question. What is left is announcement-shaped: it arrives by email or on Moodle, not in any document you already hold.

All three Which day inside exam week each final falls on. The week is fixed: 7–11 December. The days are not. Data Science 40%, Mathematics 45%, Econ of Crime 40%, all onsite. A missed final goes to the make-up week of 1–5 February 2027.
Ornaghi The real research design deadline. The syllabus says 15 December 2025 and the semester is Fall 2026. It is worth 45%.
Traxler Which paper to present, and in which session. Each student gets a different one. The session fixes the Tuesday the slides are due.
