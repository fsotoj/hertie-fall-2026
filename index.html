Wednesday is the long day: two classes back to back from 10:00 to 14:00, then a four-hour gap before the Mathematics lab at 18:00. The Thursday 16:00 lab closes that gap on its own, same room and same teaching assistant.

The two Thursday alternatives are one decision, not two. C23-Lab1 sits at 12:00, underneath the Data Science lecture, so it only becomes possible by moving that lecture to its earlier C11 slot at 10:00. Taking both gives a straight Thursday from 10:00 to 14:00 and ends Wednesday at 14:00. The Data Science lab follows the lecture slot, so it would move too, to one of the C11 groups: the Tuesday 16:00 one is already drawn above. The week then runs Tuesday to Thursday and Monday empties out.

In November none of this applies: C23-Lab1 and C23-Lab2 both meet on 19 and 26 November, inside the Chile window, where the Wednesday lab costs only the 25th.

The Data Science lab is the one series out of step. It runs Monday 31 August to 23 November with a make-up on 30 November, so each lab falls on the Monday before the Thursday session it is numbered with, and the first one ran three days before the first lecture. Both groups meet at 16:00: Lab 1 in 2.30 with Laia Domenech Burin, Lab 2 in 2.61 with Linus Hagemann. The Tuesday 16:00 group belongs to the other lecture slot and runs on Tuesday dates, 1 September to 24 November with a make-up on 1 December.
